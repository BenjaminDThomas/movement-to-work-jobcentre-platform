Introduction

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be processed when using the Movement to Work website, including its CV review, interview practice and questionnaire features.

The website is an independently developed, front-end employability platform designed to help young people build confidence, recognise their strengths and prepare for employment.

We aim to make the platform transparent and straightforward to use.

Information You May Provide

Depending on the features you choose to use, the website may process the following information:

CV Review

When using the CV Review feature, you may upload a CV containing information such as your name, contact details, qualifications, employment history and skills.

The website may process this information to identify potential improvements and generate feedback.

Interview Practice

The interview feature may process responses you provide during practice sessions, including typed answers and audio recordings where recording functionality is enabled.

This information is used to support interview preparation and provide practice feedback.

Questionnaires

When completing questionnaires, you may provide information about your interests, strengths, learning preferences, confidence and career goals.

Your responses may be used to generate a personalised employability profile and relevant suggestions.

Website Usage

Basic technical information may be processed by the website’s hosting provider to deliver the service, maintain security and diagnose technical problems.

How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into the website is used to provide the features you choose to access.

This may include:

  • Processing uploaded CVs and generating feedback.
  • Supporting interview practice and evaluating responses against predefined criteria.
  • Creating questionnaire results and personalised summaries.
  • Remembering progress and preferences where local storage is enabled.
  • Maintaining website security and functionality.

The platform does not use paid artificial intelligence services to analyse your information. Automated feedback is based on programmed rules, checks and calculations.

Local Storage and Uploaded Files

Some features may use your browser’s local storage to remember progress, questionnaire answers or other preferences.

Information saved in local storage remains available in that browser until it is cleared, deleted by the application or removed through browser settings.

Where CVs are processed entirely within your browser, their content does not need to be uploaded to a dedicated CV analysis server.

The website should not be treated as a secure storage service for sensitive personal documents. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information when testing its features.

You can clear stored website information using the available application controls, where provided, or through your browser settings.

Cookies and Website Analytics

The website may use essential browser storage to support its core functionality.

Additional analytics tools, such as Microsoft Clarity, may be introduced to understand how visitors interact with the website and identify areas for improvement.

If analytics tools are enabled, this policy will be updated to identify the provider, explain the information processed and provide details of any applicable cookies, data-sharing arrangements and retention periods.

Where consent is legally required, non-essential analytics technologies will not be activated until the appropriate consent has been obtained.

Sharing Your Information

The website is not intended to send CVs, questionnaire responses or interview recordings directly to employers, Work Coaches or recruitment organisations.

Any information you choose to share outside the website is your responsibility.

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver the pages and protect the website from attacks, Cloudflare may process limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. The website does not load fonts, scripts or analytics from any other company.

Speech to text in the interview practice is switched off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your browser converts your speech into text, and some browsers (such as Google Chrome) send the audio to their own speech service to do this. That processing is covered by your browser provider’s privacy policy. You can leave it off and type your answers instead, so nothing leaves your device.

Where additional third-party services are introduced, relevant information about those providers and any transfers of personal information will be provided in this policy.

How Long Information Is Kept

Information stored locally in your browser may remain there until you clear your browser data or use an available deletion feature.

Temporary information processed during a session may be removed when the page is refreshed or closed, depending on the feature.

Hosting providers and any enabled third-party services may have their own retention periods, which must be confirmed and disclosed before those services are used.

How Your Information Is Protected

We aim to minimise the amount of personal information processed by the website and use appropriate technical safeguards.

The platform is designed to operate without user accounts or a dedicated database for CVs and questionnaire results.

However, no website, device or internet connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid uploading documents containing unnecessary sensitive information.

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restrictions on certain uses of your information.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request transfer of information in applicable circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis for processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact the website operator using the contact details provided above.

Lawful Basis for Processing

Where UK data protection law applies, personal information must be processed under an appropriate lawful basis.

The lawful basis depends on the purpose and circumstances of each processing activity, including optional features and any third-party analytics services.

The website operator will identify and document the appropriate lawful bases for any personal information processed before the public deployment of the relevant functionality.

Use of this website does not authorise unrelated collection, disclosure or reuse of personal information.
