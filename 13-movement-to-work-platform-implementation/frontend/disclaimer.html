About This Website

The Movement to Work website is an independently developed digital prototype designed to help young people explore employability opportunities, develop practical skills and build confidence when preparing for work.

The platform provides general information about employability support, programme activities and career preparation, alongside interactive tools for CV improvement, interview practice and personal development.

This website is a demonstration project and should not be considered an official government service or an official registration portal.

Programme Information

Every effort has been made to present clear, relevant and accurate information about Movement to Work. However, programme details, eligibility criteria, availability and activities may change.

Information provided on this website should be treated as general guidance rather than confirmation of eligibility or enrolment.

For the most accurate information about participating, please speak directly to your Jobcentre Work Coach.

Joining Movement to Work

This website does not process official programme applications or guarantee participation.

If you are interested in joining Movement to Work, please contact your Jobcentre Work Coach through your Universal Credit journal or speak to them in person.

Your Work Coach can explain whether the programme is available and suitable for you, along with any requirements or next steps.

CV, Interview and Questionnaire Tools

The Get Job Ready section includes interactive features designed to help users improve their employability skills.

These tools are intended for educational purposes and general career preparation.

CV feedback, interview assessments and questionnaire results are generated through predefined rules and automated calculations. They should not be considered professional recruitment assessments or guarantees of employment.

Users are encouraged to review any suggestions carefully and seek additional guidance from their Work Coach or a qualified careers adviser where appropriate.

No Employment Guarantee

Participation in Movement to Work, or use of the website’s career preparation tools, does not guarantee employment, interviews, job offers or acceptance onto any programme.

Employment outcomes depend on individual circumstances, employer requirements and the availability of suitable opportunities.

We aim to provide an accessible and reliable user experience. However, we cannot guarantee that all information, features or interactive tools will always be available, accurate or free from technical errors.

Website content and functionality may be updated, improved or changed without prior notice.

Government and Organisational Affiliation

This website was independently designed and developed as an employability-focused project associated with work requested through Skelmersdale Jobcentre.

It should not be interpreted as an official website operated, endorsed or maintained by the Department for Work and Pensions, Jobcentre Plus or the UK Government.

Any organisational names or branding are used to provide context for the project and do not establish official endorsement.
