Purpose

Why does Movement to Work exist?

Movement to Work helps young people aged 18-30 build confidence, develop practical skills and gain experience.

It provides a supportive environment where you can learn, develop and prepare for the workplace.

The programme helps you recognise your strengths and take positive steps towards employment.

Why it matters Build confidence, gain experience and feel ready for your next step.

Movement to Work

What is Movement to Work?

Movement to Work helps young people aged 18-30 build the skills, confidence and experience they need to move towards employment.

It connects young people with opportunities to gain work experience, training and practical skills, helping break down barriers that can make getting into work difficult.

The aim is to help you build experience, recognise your strengths and feel more prepared for your next step towards work.

Our goal for you To help you feel confident, prepared and ready for work.

Also explore

Eligibility

Who is Movement to Work for?

Movement to Work is aimed at young people aged 18-30 who want to develop their skills and gain experience.

It can support those with limited or no recent work experience who want to build their confidence.

If you’re unsure about your next step or would benefit from additional support, speak to your Jobcentre adviser.

How to sign up

  • Each group has a maximum of 5 participants, keeping sessions small and supportive. This means you’ll have more opportunities to ask questions, get involved and receive personalised guidance.

  • Movement to Work is designed for young people aged 18–30 who want to build their confidence, develop practical skills and prepare for employment. Speak to your Jobcentre Work Coach to find out whether the programme is suitable for you.

  • Joining is simple! Just speak to your Jobcentre Work Coach, either in person or by sending a message through your Universal Credit journal. Let them know you’re interested in Movement to Work, and they’ll explain the next steps.

  • The programme runs over 2 weeks, with sessions taking place Monday to Wednesday each week. During this time, you’ll take part in activities designed to develop your skills, build confidence and help you prepare for employment.

  • Not at all! You don’t need any previous work experience to take part. Movement to Work is designed to help you develop new skills, discover your strengths and build the confidence to take your next step towards employment.

  • You’ll take part in practical activities, group discussions and exercises designed to develop your communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills. You’ll also receive guidance to help you understand your strengths, explore opportunities and prepare for the workplace.

  • Yes! You’ll receive support with improving your CV, preparing for interviews and understanding what employers look for. You’ll also have opportunities to practise answering common interview questions and build your confidence when applying for jobs.
