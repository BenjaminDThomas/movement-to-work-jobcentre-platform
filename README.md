**Movement to Work – Skelmersdale Jobcentre

An accessible information and guidance website requested by Skelmersdale Jobcentre to help users understand Movement to Work, explore the support available and identify suitable next steps towards employment.

**Project overview

This project involves designing and developing a responsive front-end website that Jobcentre staff can use with customers who may benefit from Movement to Work.

The website will present information in a clearer and more engaging way than a traditional information page. It will also include short interactive guidance quizzes that help users understand whether the programme may be relevant to them and where they can go for further support.

The project is being completed using a full UX process, from initial research and user-flow planning through to usability testing, high-fidelity prototyping and front-end development.

**The challenge

Employment-support information can be difficult to understand, particularly when users are unfamiliar with the programme, unsure whether it is relevant to them or uncertain about what to do next.

The challenge is to create an experience that:

*Explains Movement to Work in clear, accessible language
*Helps users understand who the programme supports
*Reduces confusion around available support and next steps
*Works well for users with different levels of digital confidence
*Supports both independent use and guided use with Jobcentre staff

**Project objectives

*Make Movement to Work easier to understand
*Present key information using a clear and accessible structure
*Provide interactive quizzes that guide users towards relevant next steps
*Create a responsive experience for desktop, tablet and mobile
*Follow accessibility best practices throughout the design and development
*Test the experience with users and improve it based on feedback
*Produce a polished UX case study and working front-end website

**Planned website features

*Clear introduction to Movement to Work

**Accessibility

Accessibility is a core requirement of the project. The final experience will aim to include:

*Clear and simple language
*Strong colour contrast
*Semantic HTML
*Keyboard-accessible navigation
*Visible focus states
*Proper form labels
*Large and clear interaction targets
*Responsive layouts
*Understandable validation and error messages
*Support for browser zoom and assistive technologies
